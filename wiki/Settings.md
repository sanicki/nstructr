## Workouts

### Sound
**Silent**, **Beeps**, **Voice** or **Coach**: see [Working out → Sound](Working-out.md#sound). Also used by the
exercise page (Voice and Coach read each step's cue once, then count).

### Full screen
Whether a workout in the browser goes full screen. The installed app is always full screen.

### Rest between exercises
Seconds of rest between one exercise and the next, in every workout. Tap − / + (1 s at a time; hold to keep
going) or type a number, from 0 to 300. Default 10. Rests between sets and between circuit rounds are set in
each workout.

## Create with AI

### AI app
The AI app **Create with AI** opens: Claude, ChatGPT, Gemini, Grok, DeepSeek, Copilot, Vibe, or another (copy only).

### Make an exercise or a workout
Opens [Create with AI](Create-with-AI.md).

## Display

### Theme
**System** (follows your phone), **Light** or **Dark**.

### Authoring mode
Adds the pose editor and JSON views, for making and fixing exercises. See
[Editing exercises](Editing-exercises.md#changing-the-poses-authoring-mode).

## Import & tools

- **Back up everything**: Export everything / Import everything. See [Backups](Sharing-and-backups.md#back-up-everything).
- **Import a file**: Choose file / Paste JSON.
- **Export your exercises** (Authoring mode): the JSON of everything in My exercises.
- **JSON format reference**: the file formats, for people writing exercises by hand.
